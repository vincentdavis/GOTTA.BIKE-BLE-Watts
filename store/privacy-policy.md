platform (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows):

- Source: [`website/privacy.html`](../website/privacy.html)

Use that URL for App Store Connect (Privacy Policy URL), Play Console
(App content › Privacy policy) and the in-app link. Edit the HTML directly;
