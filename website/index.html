Bluetooth LE cycling sensor emulator

Your phone, as a power meter.

BLE Watts makes your phone act like a standard Bluetooth cycling sensor. Cycling apps, bike computers and BLE tools pair with it like real hardware and receive the power, cadence, heart rate and speed you choose. Test your setup without pedalling a single stroke.

Download See what it does

Built for testing, not for faking rides

For developers, testers and enthusiasts who want repeatable input for head units, cycling apps and indoor-training setups. Every value it sends is simulated: the app does not measure you.

0x1818

Cycling Power

Instantaneous power plus honest crank revolution data, so receivers work out cadence exactly as they do with a crank-based power meter.

0x180D

Heart Rate

Heart rate with RR intervals. Switch it on in Settings alongside, or instead of, the power sensor.

0x1816

Speed & Cadence

Wheel speed from a speed and wheel size you set, plus crank cadence that matches the power sensor tick for tick.

Manual mode

Big plus and minus steppers for power (0 to 800 W), cadence, speed and heart rate, with up to five one-tap presets.

Test profiles

Play scripted power profiles with steps, ramps and coasts, identical on every run, with a live chart and playhead. Build your own in the editor.

Replay a ride

Import a CSV with time and power (and cadence if you have it), up to six hours, and play it back second by second.

Dual record

Connect to two real sensors at once, such as two power meters or a power meter and a smart trainer, see them side by side and record both to a CSV you can share.

CdA, Crr and mass profiles

Built-in profiles designed to help estimate the drag, rolling resistance and effective mass a virtual-riding app gives an equipment setup.

Your sensor's identity

Choose the advertised name, manufacturer and model. See how many apps are connected, and start or stop with one button.

Screenshots

The same app on every platform: a dark, glanceable screen you can read from the saddle or the workbench.

Works with standard BLE receivers

BLE Watts follows the standard Bluetooth LE sensor profiles, so it is meant for any app or bike computer that accepts standard BLE sensors. Cycling Power output has been tested with popular cycling apps and BLE debugging tools. Whether it works depends on the receiving app or device.

Responsible use. This app creates simulated sensor data for testing. Don't use it to record, upload or submit rides, races, results or leaderboard efforts as if they were real, and follow the rules of every platform you connect it to. See the Terms of Use.

Privacy, in one line

We don't collect anything. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your presets, profiles and recordings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
